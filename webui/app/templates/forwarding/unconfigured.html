No recursor is configured

Forward zones and global forwarders are a PowerDNS Recursor feature. The Authoritative Server that holds your zones cannot forward: the recursor= setting was removed in PowerDNS Authoritative 4.1.

The bundled recursor service in compose.yml sets this up. If you are running the panel against your own PowerDNS, point it at a recursor with RECURSOR_API_URL and RECURSOR_API_KEY, and set PDNS_DNS_ADDRESS to the address of the authoritative server so its zones stay answerable.
